NIGERIA SCAM-SAFETY CHECKLIST

Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or recovery codes with anyone.
Check payments inside your own bank or wallet app — not from screenshots or SMS alone.
Do not pay a “release fee” to receive a prize, job, parcel or refund without independent verification.
Contact a bank or wallet provider using the number in its official app or card.
If unsure, pause. Do not click or install anything sent by an unknown contact.
