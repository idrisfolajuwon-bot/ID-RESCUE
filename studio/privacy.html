Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use IDRIS AI STUDIO, an independent project developed under I D LABS.

Important service note

This policy describes the data flows identified in the current published Studio code and its connected services. Some service-provider retention periods and infrastructure logs are controlled by those providers and are not configured or independently verified here. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government ID numbers, confidential business information, or other sensitive information in prompts or uploads.

1. Information you provide

  • Prompts and conversations: messages you submit to AI Chat, including text and conversation context included with a request.
  • Images and documents: images you choose to send for image understanding or editing, and supported PDF or Word documents. The Studio extracts text from supported documents in your browser before including that text in an AI request. Image files can be sent to the Studio backend for image analysis or editing.
  • Account information: if you create an account, the sign-in service processes your email address, authentication information and, if supplied, your display name.
  • Saved content: items, chat history and optional AI memory that you choose to save in the Studio.
  • Support messages: information you include when emailing support.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide AI responses, generate or analyse images, support account sign-in, sync saved chats and memory across devices, maintain the Studio, understand site usage, respond to support requests and protect the service from misuse.

3. Service providers and processing

Studio requests are sent to a Cloudflare Worker backend. The current backend uses Cloudflare Workers AI models for text chat, image generation, image understanding and image editing. For some current-information questions, the backend also requests search results from Bing and GDELT. These providers process request information as needed to return results.

Account sign-in and optional cloud synchronisation use Supabase. The Studio code syncs saved conversations and optional AI memory to Supabase when you are signed in. Supabase also processes authentication and account data. The Studio loads supporting libraries from jsDelivr.

The website includes Google Analytics (measurement ID G-DXDLJLH747), which may collect website usage and device/browser information according to Google's own policies and settings.

Voice Mode uses speech-recognition and speech-synthesis features supplied by the browser or device. Their processing and availability depend on that browser/device provider.

The Video Lab's external video-generator link opens a separate third-party website. That site's privacy practices are separate from I D LABS.

4. Storage on your device and in your account

The Studio uses browser local storage to keep saved items, chat history and optional memory on the device. Clearing browser or app data may remove locally saved content. Export your Vault backup if you want to keep a copy.

If you sign in, conversations and optional AI memory can also be synchronised to your Supabase account. Signing out does not itself mean that cloud-synchronised data has been deleted.

5. Uploaded files and images

Supported PDF and DOCX files are read in your browser to extract text. That extracted text may be included in your AI chat request. Images selected for AI understanding or editing are sent to the backend for processing. Do not upload content unless you have the right to share it and are comfortable sending it to the relevant service providers.

6. Retention and deletion

Saved content stored only in your browser can be removed using the Studio's clear/delete controls or by clearing the browser's site data. Use the Studio's account controls and contact support to ask about deleting account-linked conversations or memory. A request may require verification before action.

Exact retention periods for backend requests, provider logs, account records and backups have not been confirmed in the current project configuration. Those providers may retain information under their own policies. We therefore do not promise immediate or complete deletion from every provider system or backup.

7. Cookies, analytics and external links

The Studio uses Google Analytics for usage measurement. Authentication and connected services may use browser storage or similar technologies needed to operate. External links are governed by the privacy policies of the sites they open.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used in the service architecture, including HTTPS connections to the configured services. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and use a unique password.

9. Children's privacy

IDRIS AI STUDIO is not designed to request sensitive personal information from children. If you are not old enough to independently agree to online services where you live, ask a parent or guardian to review the service and applicable provider terms before creating an account or submitting personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Studio's features, providers or data practices change. The updated date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Check this page periodically.
