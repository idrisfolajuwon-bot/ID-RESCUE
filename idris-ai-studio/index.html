ABOUT IDRIS AI STUDIO

IDRIS AI STUDIO (also written as IDRIS AI Studio or IDRIS Studio) is an independent AI workspace developed under I D LABS. It brings several creative and assistant tools into one online studio, including AI chat, voice conversations, image creation, image understanding, PDF and Word document reading, prompt building and a creation library.

IDRIS AI STUDIO is a separate project. It is not ChatGPT, is not an OpenAI product, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. The project has its own website and online service. Some features depend on internet access, supported browsers and available service capacity.
