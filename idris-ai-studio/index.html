03 / QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

What is IDRIS AI STUDIO?

An independent AI workspace developed under I D LABS for chat and creative tools.

Where can I use it?

Open the online studio using the Launch button. Some features require a supported browser and an internet connection.

Does it generate finished videos?

The Video Lab helps plan video concepts and storyboards; it does not itself render finished videos.

Is it an official OpenAI or ChatGPT product?

No. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.
